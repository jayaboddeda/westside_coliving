Key Policies

What You Should Know

📅

Lock-in Period

Minimum 2-month stay. Leaving early means paying rent for remaining lock-in days on a pro-rata basis.

📢

Notice Period

1-month notice required (after lock-in), communicated via WhatsApp. Verbal notice is not accepted.

💰

Rent Payment

Rent due by the 5th of every month. Late fee of ₹200/day applies after the 5th. Pay via net banking, cards or wallets.

🔑

Deposit Refund

Deposit refunded on the same day of exit after property inspection, minus one-time maintenance charge.

📋

Agreement

Agreements available on stamp paper (2–11 month terms). Physical copy provided on request. Stamp duty as applicable.

👥

Tenant Profile

Open to young working professionals — males, females and couples. Management reserves the right to approve applications.
