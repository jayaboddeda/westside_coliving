Our Food Policy

A few simple rules keep the cafeteria clean, safe and pleasant for everyone.

What's Included & Allowed

Lunch packed for office, ready by 7:30 AM
Meals must be consumed in the cafeteria area
Outside food is allowed in rooms
Common kitchen with microwave & appliances available

Please Avoid

Cafeteria food cannot be carried to rooms (₹2,000 charge)
Gas / electric stoves strictly prohibited (₹1,000 penalty)
These rules keep our shared spaces hygienic and pest-free for all residents.
