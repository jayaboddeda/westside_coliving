Terms & Conditions of Tenancy

Please read all terms carefully before booking. These policies ensure a great experience for all residents.

📋 Agreement Term

We offer agreement terms of 2 months to 11 months. Agreements are made on stamp paper on request. A physical copy will be shared with the tenant on request. Stamp duty charges apply as per actuals.

🔒 Lock-in Period

The lock-in period is the minimum duration of stay at our premises, which is 2 months.

If you decide to leave before the lock-in period ends, rent will be payable for the number of days remaining in the lock-in period on a pro-rata basis.

📢 Notice Period

All tenants are expected to serve one month's notice before vacating the premises. This one-month notice can be served only after completion of the two-month lock-in period.

If a tenant fails to serve the complete one-month notice period, the balance days' rent will be payable on a pro-rata basis prior to vacating.

⚠️ Notice must be given via WhatsApp Message only. Verbal communication will not be accepted.

If a new tenant occupies the vacated bed, rent will not be reimbursed. The management can also give one month's notice to vacate the property.

💰 Security Deposit

We take one month's rent as security deposit along with the running month's rent.

For advance bookings, tenants are required to pay:

  • ₹10,000 for single sharing rooms
  • ₹5,000 for twin and triple sharing rooms

This amount is non-refundable if the booking is cancelled, but is adjusted against the security deposit if you proceed.

🔄 Deposit Refund

The management will do a thorough inspection of the property for any damages. Post inspection, the security deposit will be refunded on the same day of leaving.

The management will retain the following as a one-time maintenance charge:

  • Triple Sharing: ₹1,500
  • Twin Sharing: ₹2,000
  • Single Sharing: ₹2,500
Deposit cannot be adjusted against the notice period or the last month's rent.

📅 Rent Payment

Monthly rents are payable in advance by the 5th of each month. An invoice will be sent monthly via the community app. Payment accepted via net banking, debit card, credit card or online wallet. HRA-compliant rental receipts are provided every month.

⚠️ Late fee: ₹200/day for payments after the 5th of the month. If rent is not received by the 15th, a 7-day eviction notice will be issued.

🏠 Alterations, Theft & Damage

Alterations to any electrical or furnished fittings are not allowed. Theft and damage will be penalised as per the actual value of the products in question.

In the case of theft/loss or damage of any furnishing or appliance, all tenants staying at the premises at that time may be held responsible. The recovery amount will be deducted from security deposits. If it exceeds the deposit, tenants are liable to pay the balance.

Lost keys: ₹500 charge per lost key.

📦 Room Booking

Schedule a property visit to see the rooms first. After selecting your room, payment and agreement details will be sent via email. A room is only confirmed upon payment.

🧹 Housekeeping

Housekeeping services include:

  1. Sweeping and mopping of the premises (except Sundays & National Holidays)
  2. Cleaning of bathrooms (except Sundays & National Holidays)
  3. Cleaning of doors and window panes
  4. Dusting of furniture

🤝 Community Rules

Residents are expected to respect the privacy of other housemates and keep noise levels within the tolerance of others. Quiet hours must be maintained after 11:00 PM.

All residents are expected to adhere to house rules. The management reserves the right to expel a resident for house rule violations.

🚭 Smoking, Alcohol & Illegal Substances

Smoking and alcohol are not allowed inside rooms or common areas. A designated smoking area is available at the parking.

⚠️ Other banned substances are strictly prohibited. Illegal activities will be reported to the authorities. Violation results in immediate eviction with 30 minutes' notice.

🛡️ Safety Notice

Residents' safety and wellbeing are our top priority. If you experience or witness any form of harassment, abuse or inappropriate behaviour within the premises, please report it immediately to management. If you have concerns or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out.

🧳 Personal Belongings

The tenant is solely responsible for their belongings. Neither the management nor its vendors shall be held liable for any loss or theft.

The management keeps an extra key for maintenance purposes and for showing the premises to prospective tenants. All tenants are advised to keep their valuables locked at all times.

👥 Guest Policy

Guest visitors are not allowed inside rooms except for parents and spouses (marriage proof document required). The host resident is responsible for their guest's behaviour.

Overnight stays for visitors are at the discretion of the management. All other visitors must meet in the common lobby area.

🍛 Food Policy

Meal timings: Breakfast 7:30–10:30 AM | Lunch 1:00–2:30 PM | Dinner 7:30–10:00 PM. Lunch packs for office are ready by 7:30 AM.

Cafeteria food is not allowed in rooms. Maintenance charge of ₹2,000 applies if food is carried to rooms. Outside food is permitted in rooms — please clean up and dispose of waste properly.

Usage of gas or electric stoves in rooms is strictly prohibited. ₹1,000 penalty if found. All electrical appliances are available in the common kitchen area. Please clean utensils after use. Unhygienic usage of the common kitchen attracts a ₹500 maintenance charge.

👕 Laundry Policy

Laundry is picked up by external vendors twice a week. Maximum of 6 garments per pickup — excess will be returned unwashed. Laundry includes both washing and ironing. This service is included in the monthly rent.

⚡ Electricity Bill Policy

All rooms have prepaid electricity meters. Each tenant receives their own login credentials to recharge their specific room meter. Electricity is charged based on actual usage and is not included in rent. Unused meter balance will not be refunded upon vacating.

❌ Agreement Termination

The management reserves the right to terminate an agreement based on conduct or violation of any of the policies set by the management.

Questions About These Terms?

If you have any questions or need clarification on any policy, please contact us before signing.
