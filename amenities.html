World-Class Facilities

Premium Amenities Included

Everything you need for a comfortable, productive and enjoyable life — all included in your monthly rent at Westside Coliving.

In Your Room

Fully Furnished & Move-In Ready

Every room at Westside comes fully equipped so you can move in on Day 1 without buying a single piece of furniture.

❄️

Air Conditioning

Every room is fully air-conditioned — stay cool and comfortable throughout the year, even during peak Hyderabad summers.

📺

Television

A television in every room for your entertainment and relaxation after a long day at work.

🚿

Geyser / Water Heater

Hot water available at any time of the day with individual geysers in rooms — no waiting, no sharing.

🚪

Wardrobe

Spacious wardrobe in every room for all your clothing, accessories and personal storage needs.

💼

Work Table & Chair

A dedicated ergonomic work table and chair to help you stay productive whether you're working from home or just unwinding.

🧊

Refrigerator

A refrigerator in every room to store your personal items, beverages and snacks throughout the day.

Common Areas

Shared Spaces Designed for Living

Our thoughtfully designed common areas help you stay active, social and productive without leaving home.

📶

High-Speed WiFi

Fast and reliable internet throughout the property — perfect for remote work, video calls and streaming.

💧

Water Dispenser

Purified drinking water available 24/7 in common areas. Stay hydrated without any extra cost.

💻

Common Workspace

A dedicated co-working space for focused work, study sessions or collaborative projects with fellow residents.

🏋️

Gym

An in-house gym so you never have to skip your workout. Stay fit and healthy without an extra gym membership.

🏓

TT Table (Table Tennis)

Unwind and socialise with a game of table tennis in our recreation area — great for stress relief and bonding.

🎯

Foosball & Carrom

Multiple indoor games available for evening entertainment and community bonding.

Services Included

We Handle the Chores

Your time is valuable. Our services are designed to eliminate daily hassles so you can focus on what matters.

🧹

Daily Housekeeping

Rooms and washrooms cleaned every day (except Sundays & national holidays). Bedsheets changed regularly. Dust-free, hygienic living guaranteed.

👕

Laundry Service

Pickup twice a week (max 6 garments per pickup). Includes both wash and ironing. Returned clean and pressed — no laundromat trips needed.

🍛

3 Meals Daily

Breakfast, lunch and dinner prepared fresh daily in our in-house kitchen. Diverse weekly menu covering South Indian, North Indian and more.

⚡

24/7 Power Backup

Uninterrupted power backup ensures your work, comfort and appliances are never affected by power outages.

🍳

Common Kitchen

Equipped with microwave, sandwich maker, egg boiler and bread toaster for additional cooking needs. Clean after use.

🔧

Maintenance Support

Lodge a complaint via email or WhatsApp and get a response within 24 hours. Emergency support available via the property manager.

⚡

Electricity Policy

All rooms are equipped with prepaid electricity meters. Each resident receives their own login credentials to recharge their specific room meter. Electricity is charged separately based on actual usage. Unused meter balance is non-refundable upon vacating.
